## Priority issues
- [P0] Paste from Excel: preview collapses to 2 px at 1366×657, sticky th inside dialog, column detector can swap name/type (60 comps named "Table"), no in-paste dedupe. Fix: min-height preview, static th in dialog, decide name column first, no fuzzy match <4 chars, dedupe, show column mapping with swap. (/impeccable harden)
- [P1] Too much fixed chrome: 8 rows on load, 175/657 px sticky after scroll. Fix: hub bar/tabs scroll away or focus mode, one-line title+pase+fases, one-line checks with +N, Comentario as icon. Target ≥17 on load, ≥22 after scroll. (/impeccable layout)
- [P1] Close preparation incoherent: summary omits warnings (Sin paquete comps), user asserts "Todo correcto", page stays editable after "bloqueada", Reabrir without confirm, Streetref add reopens checks, stale toasts. Fix: Avisos block + "Cerrar con N avisos", read-only after close, confirm reopen, dismiss stale toasts. (/impeccable harden)
- [P2] Colour semantics collide; Canary overloaded; 4 px Canary stripe = accent as line in light; dark tab underline Mandarin. Fix: Canary only blockers, neutral dashed for warnings, projects as outline tags/dots, REQ other colour, Lime tab. (/impeccable colorize)
- [P2] Keyboard/focus: 38 tabs to first row, 229 to bulk bar, invisible cell focus, dialogs don't trap/restore focus, dark placeholders, 15–17 px targets. Fix: 2 px focus ring, bulk bar earlier in DOM or shortcut, focus trap, ::placeholder colour, 24 px hit areas. (/impeccable audit, /impeccable polish)
